# TrustLedger — Citations & Benchmark Sources

This document catalogues all external industry figures, survey statistics, and empirical benchmarks cited across TrustLedger's web surfaces, presentation pitch deck, and documentation.

---

## 1. Market Insights Industry Size

- **Claim on surface**: "USD 150B+ a year spent on market insights (ESOMAR, 2024 data)" / "The market-insights industry represents USD 150B+ annually (ESOMAR, 2024 data)."
- **Scope**: Total global insights industry turnover, encompassing research projects (USD 56B), software and analytics platforms (USD 62B), and reporting/consultancy (USD 35B). It does not represent survey spending alone or TrustLedger revenue.
- **Source**: ESOMAR *Global Market Research 2025* report; Research World, "Inside the $153bn insights industry".
- **Date**: 4 November 2025 (reporting on 2024 full-year market data).
- **URL**: [https://researchworld.com/articles/inside-the-153bn-insights-industry](https://researchworld.com/articles/inside-the-153bn-insights-industry)

## 2. Online Survey Panel Fraud & Reliability

- **Claim on surface**: "In a Pew survey fielded Nov 2024 (11,114 US adults), 18% of cases were flagged (Pew Research Center, 27 Aug 2026)."
- **Scope**: Empirical testing of online opt-in polling respondents against trap and consistency check questions (e.g. fictitious social media platforms or extreme implausible qualifications). 1,963 out of 11,114 cases (18%) answered "Yes" to one or more trap questions and were flagged. Phrased strictly as "flagged", not "proven fake".
- **Source**: Pew Research Center, *"No Easy Fix for Bogus Respondents in Online Opt-In Polls"*.
- **Fielded Date**: 14–19 November 2024.
- **Publication Date**: 27 August 2026.
- **URL**: [https://www.pewresearch.org/methods/2026/08/27/no-easy-fix-for-bogus-respondents-in-online-opt-in-polls/](https://www.pewresearch.org/methods/2026/08/27/no-easy-fix-for-bogus-respondents-in-online-opt-in-polls/)

## 3. Global Counterfeit Trade Scale

- **Claim on surface**: "USD 467 billion in counterfeit goods traded in a year (2021 data) · up to 2.3% of world trade · up to 4.7% of EU imports."
- **Scope**: Economic magnitude of international trade in counterfeit and pirated products.
- **Source**: OECD & European Union Intellectual Property Office (EUIPO), *Mapping Global Trade in Fakes 2025*.
- **Date**: May 2025 (latest available comprehensive measurement year: 2021).
- **URL**: [https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2025/05/global-trade-in-fake-goods-reached-USD-467-billion-posing-risks-to-consumer-safety-and-compromising-intellectual-property.html](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2025/05/global-trade-in-fake-goods-reached-USD-467-billion-posing-risks-to-consumer-safety-and-compromising-intellectual-property.html)
- **Report PDF**: [https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/05/mapping-global-trade-in-fakes-2025_5c812e3c/94d3b29f-en.pdf](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/05/mapping-global-trade-in-fakes-2025_5c812e3c/94d3b29f-en.pdf)

## 4. Smartphone Sector Counterfeit Impact

- **Claim on surface**: "Smartphones alone: 12.9% of sales lost, ≈ EUR 45.3 billion (2015 data)."
- **Scope**: Direct and indirect sales lost by legitimate smartphone manufacturers due to counterfeit devices.
- **Source**: EUIPO & International Telecommunication Union (ITU), *The Economic Cost of IPR Infringement in the Smartphones Sector*.
- **Date**: 2017 (analyzing 2015 sector data).
